[h1]奢侈品奥克莫吸注射器 / Luxury Nomore Injector[/h1]

[b]一支奥克莫吸注射器要走完整条化工链，最后只卖 50 信用点——这不合理。[/b]

做一支要经过：梦帽菇 → 菇糊 → 45°C 萃取出梦帽菇萃取液 → 除杂、过滤 → 按 1:1 配还原剂、加脂合剂催化、全程 45–55°C → 得到奥克莫吸 → 再过滤、蒸发 → 每 4 毫升才成型一支。

更要命的是它在设定里的位置：奥克西莫合法、好用，也危险——处方开得太随便，原版里甚至有「奥克西莫成瘾情况日益严重」的站内新闻事件，各诊所的成瘾与过量病例一起激增。奥克莫吸正是它的[b]非成瘾替代品[/b]：有效镇痛，没有依赖，也没有过量风险。
结果这样一支优秀的原型药，只需要：50。

而这支药在设定里是李北文拿命在推的原型药：他被大药企栽赃成「持械的卡特尔制毒师」，人头挂着 400 信用点悬赏，卖的就是它。

[h2]它做了什么[/h2]
[list]
[*]给「奥克莫吸注射器」（`pink_alt_injector`）加上[b]奢侈品[/b]类型，和赛纳普斯注射器同档。
[*]基础价值 [b]50 → 350[/b]，与赛纳普斯注射器一致。
[*]买奢侈品的客人与收藏家会开始求购它；展示柜的奢侈品统计也把它算进去。
[*]配方、纯度、药效、剂量、合法性一律不动，只改类型和价格。
[/list]

[h2]为什么是 350[/h2]
[list]
[*]它的工序比大多数成品药都长：梦帽菇萃取链 + 还原剂还原 + 脂合剂催化，还要控温、除杂、蒸发。
[*]它是奥克西莫的非成瘾替代品，而奥克西莫的成瘾与过量在设定里已经泛滥；这种替代品如果没有任何溢价，等于在惩罚玩家所以没人愿意做它。
[*]50 信用点连原料都盖不住；350 是原版已有的价格锚点（赛纳普斯注射器），不是凭空造数。
[*]李北文剧情里「全部买下打五折」：原版 8 支打包才 400，和他人头悬赏一样多，玩家没理由买；改后 8 支 2800、五折 1400，才像一笔「通缉犯靠卖样品筹钱」的交易。
[/list]

[h2]对玩家来说也是亏本买卖[/h2]
拿同样的 100 ml 梦帽菇萃取液算一遍：
[list]
[*][b]做梦尘[/b]：有效成分 100 ml，99% 纯度收入约 3,636 信用点，加 15% 零售加价约 3,939；梦尘是[b]中级违禁品[/b]。
[*][b]做奥克莫吸[/b]：1 ml 萃取液 + 1 ml 还原剂只出 0.6 ml，100 ml 萃取液只剩 60 ml；每支还要 4 ml，等于同样原料只出 15 支，99% 纯度收入约 1,788，加价后约 2,045。
[*]（90% / 80% / 60% / 45% 纯度下同样成立：梦尘 3,167 / 2,688 / 2,583 / 1,889，奥克莫吸 1,533 / 1,313 / 1,250 / 933。）
[*]而且奥克莫吸还要额外吃掉 100 ml 还原剂：收益更低、成本更高，还是两者里唯一不违法的那个，结果却是最便宜的那个。
[/list]
所以 50 信用点不是「便宜」，是「没人做」：玩家只要算一遍，就该转身去做梦尘。

[h2]注意[/h2]
[list]

[*]只动这一件物品，其他注射器、其他药品原样。
[/list]

--- English ---

[h1]Luxury Nomore Injector[/h1]

[b]A Nomore Injector takes half a chemistry chain to make, and the game sells it for 50 credits. That is not a fair price.[/b]

Spore cap → mash → 45°C extraction into dream-cap extract → filter out the impurities → blend 1:1 with reducing agent and catalyse with lipid binder at 45–55°C → Nomore → filter and evaporate → 4 ml per finished injector.

Worse, look at what it is in the setting: Oxymore is legal, effective and dangerous — prescribed far too freely, so freely that the game itself runs an "Oxymore Addiction On The Rise" headline event while clinics drown in addiction and overdose cases. Nomore is its [b]non-addictive replacement[/b]: real analgesia, no dependence, no overdose risk.
And yet this excellent prototype is priced at just 50.

In the fiction this is the prototype Li Beiwen is pushing at the cost of his life: framed by a pharma giant as an armed cartel chemist, with a 400-credit bounty on his head, and this is what he sells.

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*]Adds the [b]Luxury Item[/b] type to the Nomore Injector (`pink_alt_injector`), the same tier as the Synaprest Injector.
[*]Raises its base value from [b]50 to 350[/b], matching the Synaprest Injector.
[*]Luxury buyers and collectors start asking for it, and shop showcases count it as a luxury item.
[*]Recipe, purity, effect, dosage and legality are untouched — only the type and the price change.
[/list]

[h2]Why 350[/h2]
[list]
[*]The item costs more work than most finished medicines: a full dream-cap extraction chain plus reducing agent and a lipid-binder catalysis step.
[*]It is the non-addictive answer to Oxymore, a drug whose addiction and overdose problem runs through the whole station; with no price premium on it, the game is punishing the player, and nobody bothers to make it.
[*]At 50 it does not even cover the feedstock. 350 is the price already used by the Synaprest Injector, so nothing new is invented.
[*]Li Beiwen offers his stock "all of it, half price". Vanilla: eight injectors for 400, exactly his bounty, so there is no reason to buy. At 350: 2800, or 1400 at half price — a trade that reads like a wanted man funding his research.
[/list]

[h2]It is a loss for the player too[/h2]
[list]
[*]Take the same 100 ml of Dream Cap Extract. [b]Dream Dust[/b]: 100 ml of product, about 3,636 credits at 99% purity (3,939 with the 15% retail markup) — and it is [b]mid-tier contraband[/b].
[*][b]Nomore[/b]: 1 ml extract + 1 ml reducing agent yields only 0.6 ml, so 100 ml of extract becomes 60 ml; four millilitres per injector means 15 injectors, about 1,788 credits at 99% purity (2,045 with markup) — plus 100 ml of reducing agent burned.
[*]Same pattern at every purity (90 / 80 / 60 / 45%: Dream Dust 3,167 / 2,688 / 2,583 / 1,889 versus Nomore 1,533 / 1,313 / 1,250 / 933).
[*]Lower revenue, higher input cost, and it is the one that is not even contraband — yet it is the cheaper of the two. At 50 credits this is not a bargain, it is dead content: anyone who does the math goes and makes Dream Dust instead.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Injectors already sitting in an old save keep the old price: the value is stored on the item instance, so only newly created or newly crafted ones cost 350.
[*]Only this one item is changed; every other injector and medicine is untouched.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Game version 0.46D. One Harmony patch on `MedsItemDirectory.Injector` that only rewrites this item's return value.
[*]No capabilities declared (files / network / steam); no new items, no recipe table edits, no save format changes.
[*]If another mod also reprices this item, the patch that runs last wins.
[/list]
