[h1]奢侈品奥克莫吸注射器 / Luxury Nomore Injector[/h1]

[b]一支奥克莫吸注射器要走完整条化工链，最后只卖 50 信用点——这不合理。[/b]

梦帽菇 → 菇糊 → 45°C 萃取 → 除杂、过滤 → 按 1:1 配还原剂、加脂合剂催化、全程 45–55°C → 再过滤、蒸发 → 每 4 毫升才成型一支。

它在设定里是奥克西莫的[b]非成瘾替代品[/b]：有效镇痛、没有依赖、没有过量风险；也是李北文拿命在推的原型药——他被大药企栽赃成制毒师，人头挂着 400 信用点悬赏，卖的就是它。而这样一支药，原版定价 50，比原料还便宜。

[h2]它做了什么[/h2]
[list]
[*]给「奥克莫吸注射器」（`pink_alt_injector`）加上[b]奢侈品[/b]类型，和赛纳普斯注射器同档。
[*]基础价值按倍数加价：[b]50 × 倍数[/b]，默认 4 倍＝200。
[*]倍数是个[b]设置[/b]（模组设置里 1 到 10 倍）：想要 7 倍＝350 的老价位，或者更轻的 2 倍＝100，自己调。
[*]买奢侈品的客人与收藏家会开始求购它；展示柜的奢侈品统计也把它算进去。
[*]配方、纯度、药效、剂量、合法性一律不动，只改类型和价格。
[/list]

[h2]为什么加价、为什么默认 4 倍[/h2]
[list]
[*]它的工序比大多数成品药都长，50 信用点连原料都盖不住，玩家算一遍就转身去做梦尘了。
[*]350（7 倍）是原版已有的价格锚点（赛纳普斯注射器），不是凭空造数。
[*]但 1.0.x 固定 7 倍，配上纯度加成后一支上等针管能卖到近 700，玩家反馈偏重，所以 1.1.0 默认降到 4 倍，并把倍率开放成设置。
[/list]

[h2]对玩家来说也是亏本买卖[/h2]
拿同样的 100 ml 梦帽菇萃取液算一遍：
[list]
[*][b]做梦尘[/b]：99% 纯度收入约 3,636 信用点，加 15% 零售加价约 3,939；梦尘是[b]中级违禁品[/b]。
[*][b]做奥克莫吸[/b]：1 ml 萃取液 + 1 ml 还原剂只出 0.6 ml，100 ml 只剩 60 ml；每支 4 ml，等于只出 15 支，99% 纯度约 1,788，加价后约 2,045，还要多烧 100 ml 还原剂。
[*]收益更低、成本更高，还是两者里唯一不违法的那个——结果它最便宜。
[/list]

[h2]注意[/h2]
[list]
[*]老存档里已经存在的注射器仍是原价：价格存在物品实例上，只有新生成或新合成的才按当前倍数计价。
[*]改倍数只影响之后新生成的针管，已经造出来的不会跟着变。
[/list]

[h2]兼容性[/h2]
[list]
[*]游戏版本 0.46D。一个 Harmony 补丁（`MedsItemDirectory.Injector`），只改这一件物品的返回值。
[*]不声明任何能力；不新增物品、不改配方表、不碰存档结构。
[/list]

[h1]Luxury Nomore Injector[/h1]

[b]A Nomore Injector takes half a chemistry chain to make, and the game sells it for 50 credits.[/b]

Spore cap → mash → 45°C extraction → filter → blend 1:1 with reducing agent and catalyse with lipid binder at 45–55°C → filter and evaporate → 4 ml per finished injector.

In the fiction it is the [b]non-addictive replacement[/b] for Oxymore: real analgesia, no dependence, no overdose risk — and the prototype Li Beiwen is pushing at the cost of his life, framed as a cartel chemist with a 400-credit bounty on his head. It costs 50.

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*]Adds the [b]Luxury Item[/b] type to the Nomore Injector (`pink_alt_injector`), the same tier as the Synaprest Injector.
[*]Applies a markup over the base value: [b]50 x multiplier[/b], 4x by default (200).
[*]The multiplier is a [b]mod setting[/b] (1x to 10x): pick the old 7x = 350, or a lighter 2x = 100.
[*]Luxury buyers and collectors start asking for it, and showcases count it as a luxury item.
[*]Recipe, purity, effect, dosage and legality are untouched — only the type and the price change.
[/list]

[h2]Why a markup, and why 4x by default[/h2]
[list]
[*]It costs more work than most finished medicines; at 50 credits it does not even cover the feedstock, so anyone who does the math makes Dream Dust instead.
[*]350 (7x) is the price already used by the Synaprest Injector, so nothing new is invented.
[*]But 1.0.x was a flat 7x, which with the purity bonus pushed a top-grade injector towards 700 credits; players said that was too much, so 1.1.0 defaults to 4x and exposes the multiplier as a setting.
[/list]

[h2]It is a loss for the player too[/h2]
Take 100 ml of Dream Cap Extract:
[list]
[*][b]Dream Dust[/b]: about 3,636 credits at 99% purity (3,939 with the 15% markup) — and it is [b]mid-tier contraband[/b].
[*][b]Nomore[/b]: 1 ml extract + 1 ml reducing agent yields 0.6 ml, so 100 ml becomes 60 ml; 4 ml per injector means 15 injectors, about 1,788 credits at 99% purity (2,045 with markup), plus 100 ml of reducing agent burned.
[*]Lower revenue, higher input cost, and it is the one that is not even contraband — yet it is the cheaper of the two.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Injectors already in an old save keep the old price: the value is stored on the item instance, so only newly created ones use the current multiplier.
[*]Changing the multiplier only affects injectors created afterwards.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Game version 0.46D. One Harmony patch on `MedsItemDirectory.Injector` that only rewrites this item's return value.
[*]No capabilities declared; no new items, no recipe edits, no save format changes.
[/list]
