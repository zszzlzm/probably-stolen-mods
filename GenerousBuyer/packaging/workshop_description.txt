[h1]删除种类多样 / No More Variety[/h1]

[b]用上这个模组把要求种类多样的顾客每一分钱都掏干净吧。[/b]

种类多样标签的顾客是不是很气人，明明手里面那么多预算，
但是他每种物品只买一件导致你手里面那么多货但是根本从顾客手里面掏不出来他的钱。

这个模组只做一件事：把「只买一件」拿掉，把剩下的一半留下。

[h2]改了什么[/h2]
[list]
[*]规则名从「种类多样」改成「慷慨」；说明文字同步改写。
[*]客人不再因为「已经买过同一种物品」而拒收——同一个物品可以连续卖给他，不限次数。
[*]柜台加成保留：你放上柜台的每件物品仍然 +15% 价值，改名为「慷慨买家加成」。
[*]商店的其它规则、其它客人、价格系统、存档结构，一律不动。
[/list]

[h2]为什么不是直接删掉这条规则[/h2]
[list]
[*]+15% 是实打实的收益，删掉规则等于把这半条也扔了。
[*]「只买一件」是限制玩家的那一半：它不增加任何趣味，只是让你明明有货却卖不出他的钱。
[*]改的是判定本身，不是 UI 遮罩：拒收、行内提示、摆上柜台、能否出售四处判定一起放开，不会出现「显示能卖、点下去被拒」这种半修状态。
[/list]

[h2]注意[/h2]
[list]
[*]只影响带这条规则的客人，其它客人完全不受影响。
[*]不写入存档，卸载后规则立刻恢复原版。
[/list]

[h2]兼容性[/h2]
[list]
[*]游戏版本 0.46D。两个 Harmony 补丁：一个关掉重复购买判定，一个替换四条界面文本。
[*]不声明任何能力（files / network / steam）；不新增物品、不改配方、不碰存档结构。
[*]和其它商店/客人类 mod 的冲突概率很低；如果别的 mod 也重命名这条规则，看谁的补丁后执行。
[/list]

[h2]安装[/h2]
订阅后在「模组」菜单里启用，然后重启游戏一次。

--- English ---

[h1]No More Variety[/h1]

[b]Use this mod to squeeze every last credit out of the customers who ask for variety.[/b]

Annoying, aren't they? They walk in with a fat budget, but they only ever buy one copy of each item —
so you sit on a warehouse full of stock and still cannot get their money out of their pocket.

This mod does one thing: it removes the "one copy only" part and keeps the other half.

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*]Renames the rule from "Variety" to "Generous" and rewrites its description.
[*]The customer no longer refuses an item just because they already bought one: the same item can be sold to them again and again, with no limit.
[*]The counter bonus stays: every item you place still gets +15% value, now called the Generous Buyer Bonus.
[*]Nothing else changes: no other rules, no other customers, no pricing, no save format.
[/list]

[h2]Why not just delete the rule[/h2]
[list]
[*]The +15% is real money; deleting the rule would throw that half away too.
[*]"One of each" is the half that only costs the player: it adds no interesting decision, it just leaves you holding stock you cannot sell to them.
[*]The patch disables the check itself rather than hiding UI, so all four call sites (refusal, row label, placing on the counter, can-sell test) lift together — no half-patched "looks sellable but gets refused" state.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Only customers with this rule are affected; everyone else behaves exactly as before.
[*]No save data is written; disabling the mod restores the vanilla rule immediately.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Game version 0.46D. Two Harmony patches: one disables the duplicate-refusal check, one swaps four UI strings.
[*]No capabilities declared (files / network / steam); no new items, no recipe edits, no save changes.
[*]If another mod also renames this rule, the patch that runs last wins.
[/list]

[h2]Install[/h2]
Subscribe, enable it in the Mods menu, then restart the game once.
